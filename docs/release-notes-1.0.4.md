**Free forever, Pro when you want more.** Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.

**What's new in 1.0.4**

- **No more demo — DualShift is free.** The bridge, button mapping, dead zones, gyro aiming, adaptive triggers, the light bar and rumble work without any time limit.
- **DualShift Pro.** Mouse mode, keyboard keys from the controller (Alt+Tab and the rest), the on-screen keyboard, turbo and per-game profiles are Pro. They are on for the first 7 days; after that DualShift keeps working as Free and the Pro cards lock until you enter a key. Pro is a one-time **500 Telegram Stars** — open the new **DualShift Pro** page and press **Buy**.
- **A new look.** Icons in the sidebar, a highlight that slides to the page you pick, pages that fade in, and a ripple on every button you press. Gold marks everything that is Pro, and an **Administrator** badge shows when DualShift runs elevated.
- **Stick drift check.** On the Axes page: leave the sticks alone for three seconds, and DualShift measures their drift at rest and sets the dead zones for you.
- **Light bar effects.** *Breathing* slowly dims and brightens your colour, *Rainbow* cycles through every colour.

Keys bought earlier stay valid and unlock Pro.

**Installing**

Run `DualShift Setup.exe`. Windows shows a SmartScreen warning — the installer is not code-signed yet: **More info → Run anyway**. Agree to install the bundled ViGEmBus driver when the installer offers it; without it Windows cannot create the virtual controller.

Already have an earlier version? Install on top — your settings, profiles and key stay. If you had **DualShift Demo**, the installer removes it and puts the regular version in its place.
